Reset this site

Forgets one site: its Auto / Always / Never setting, its Smart or Simple mode and every fix you saved there. That site follows the global default again. Nothing else changes, and no other site is touched.

Hard reset

A hard reset erases, with nothing kept:

  • every setting — the master switch, the look and its three sliders, image dimming, and what new sites start as;
  • your whole site list, with every Auto / Always / Never and Smart / Simple choice in it;
  • every fix you saved with the picker, on every site;
  • the schedule, and the timer behind it;
  • all per-tab state — open tabs are cleaned and put back on the defaults without a reload.

A hard reset cannot be undone. There is no copy to go back to. The only step beyond it is removing Darkrai at chrome://extensions and installing it again.

Shortcut: Alt+Shift+D toggles the current site. Change it at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

Hard reset everything?

This erases every setting, your whole site list, every saved picker fix, the schedule and all per-tab state.

It cannot be undone. Removing and reinstalling Darkrai is the only step beyond it.
