# image-rendering: pixelated, where it fills about 86% of the screen's
# height. Original pixel fan art of Darkrai: the white flame-like plume
# sweeping up and to the viewer's right (the largest bright area), a small
# black head under its base with one ice-blue eye on the viewer's left, the

# ragged cloak with three points and no legs, thin arms held a little out.

#   node tools\icons\build-icons.js --sprites-only
